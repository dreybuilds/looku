# Inspiration sites for looku

Start with Whering for the wardrobe, Vogue Runway for the editorial direction, and Kredete for Wallet. These are design references. The [Kenyan retailer application list](KENYA-APPLICATIONS.md) tracks commercial partnerships separately.

| Reference | What to look at | How to use it in looku |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Whering](https://whering.co.uk/) | Its tour moves from photographing clothes to organising a wardrobe and receiving outfit suggestions. | After adding a garment, keep it visible and give the shopper a direct path to styling it. |
| [Indyx](https://www.myindyx.com/) | A digital closet is the starting point for styling clothes someone already owns. | A saved look should let someone see the pieces together again the next time they get dressed. |
| [Vogue Runway](https://www.vogue.com/fashion-shows) | Designer collections group individual looks into a visual story. | Build each looku edit around a recognisable palette, one full outfit and a fabric detail. Use original looku imagery. |
| [Enda](https://www.endasportswear.com/) | Kenyan running shoes and apparel give an outfit a specific setting and purpose. | Include Kenyan references when planning sporty looks and footwear portraits. |
| [Oak Apparel](https://oakapparel.co.ke/partners) | Nairobi graphic tees and artist storefronts connect clothing with its maker. | Give a local brand or artist credit alongside a featured piece and link to its actual storefront. |
| [LTK](https://www.shopltk.com/) | Creator-led discovery connects a whole look with shopping. | Show the outfit first and offer a purchase only for a specific missing piece. Label the retailer and referral relationship. |
| [Kredete](https://kredete.io/) | Accounts, spending and savings are presented as distinct actions. | Bring a shopper directly to the wallet action they asked for, carry their spending limit through setup, and surface the receipt after payment. |

**Walkthrough to repeat when reviewing a release.** Add a photo with a wardrobe filter active. Type a look, change its palette, build it, and save it. Reopen the outfit after reloading. Check an over-budget jacket, go to Wallet, create or restore a test wallet, then return to the same limit. Explicitly approve a purchase only when ready. The receipt should show the shopper’s payment separately from the boutique’s referral payout.
