# looku affiliate directory

Last checked November 3, 2026. The directory covers 26 publicly documented programs across Kenyan retail, African fashion and international stores. Each link opens the merchant’s or network’s program information. looku still needs merchant approval and its own publisher identifiers to generate commission-earning product links. The region describes the program page or market. Delivery to a shopper’s address and acceptance of a Kenya-based publisher must be checked individually.

For the first local footwear integration, VaaBay has the clearest documented path from application to a tracking link. For a broader catalogue, Mytheresa explicitly offers a product feed after approval. Both require confirming that looku’s styling app and its intended placement of links qualify. ## From a program listing to a payable referral

1. Register looku as the publisher with the merchant or its official network. Confirm allowed channels, geography, catalog access, and rules for displaying product data or using shopping agents. Archive the approved terms and commission schedule.
2. Import merchant-issued product URLs or generate deep links through the approved network. Show the disclosure beside a recommendation before the shopper leaves looku. Only add an opaque click reference where the network permits it.
3. Reconcile the merchant’s signed event or authenticated report with the click reference and order ID. Record pending, approved, reversed, and paid commissions separately. Preserve the reported currency, amount, and payout reference. A product click does not demonstrate a sale.
4. Keep the Stellar testnet boutique in its own ledger. The public retail programs above provide no evidence that they accept x402 checkout or settle affiliate commissions on Stellar. Enable a real merchant’s Stellar path only after an explicit integration agreement.

Public search cannot enumerate every private program, invite-only deal or affiliate dashboard. This directory covers first-party pages located during this search. No applications were submitted, private catalogue feeds accessed or personal affiliate links generated.
